NextGen Privacy & Youth Safety Policy

Effective Date: January 2026 | Organization: Indaba Men's Corner (Pietermaritzburg, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa)

Our Core Commitment to Young People

NextGen is built from the ground up to protect children, teens, and young adults. We do not sell personal data, do not display third-party advertisements, and maintain zero public listings of minors.

1. Data Collection & Usage

  • Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Used strictly on-device to compute real-time GPS coordinates when you trigger the Family SOS function. Coordinates are passed exclusively to your designated emergency contact. Location data is not tracked or stored on any central server.
  • SMS Permission (SEND_SMS): Used solely to dispatch emergency location alerts directly to your saved parent, guardian, or family member during distress.
  • Contacts Access (READ_CONTACTS): Used solely on-device to let you select an emergency contact and discover friends already in your address book. NextGen never uploads or scrapes your address book.
  • Personal Goals & Journaling: Your reflections, streak counters, and Rand budget records remain stored locally on your device via encrypted preferences.

2. Child & Youth Protection

  • No Public Profiles: Minors cannot be searched, discovered, or contacted by strangers.
  • No Commercial Profiling: We do not track user behavior to serve targeted marketing.
  • Verified Helpline Triage: All crisis helplines (112, Childline 116, SADAG 0800 567 567) are verified South African public and NPO resources.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Since data is held locally on your device, clearing the app's cache or uninstalling NextGen completely deletes your local files and settings.
